# Demo audio credits

The before/after demo and the intelligibility score use these recordings. The
"before" audio is a simulation: the clean recordings are run through a virtual
theater by `make_demo.py`.

- **Voice:** *Sense and Sensibility*, chapter 18, by Jane Austen, read by Heather
  Barnett. LibriSpeech (https://www.openslr.org/12/), CC BY 4.0.
- **Score:** *Hungarian Dance No. 5* by Johannes Brahms, performed by the US Army
  Strings (via Musopen). Public domain.
- `score.py` also uses two more LibriSpeech readings (CC BY 4.0): *The Age of Chivalry*,
  chapter IX, by Thomas Bulfinch, read by Anders Lankford, and *The Ashiel Mystery* by
  Marion Bryce, read by Garth Comira.

All of these are in the librosa sample data: https://github.com/librosa/data
